Wall Rental Questions, Answered

How much can I earn renting my wall for a mural?

It varies with the wall. Walls on Beautify have rented for as little as $1,000 a month and as much as $50,000 a month, usually for one to three months per campaign. Bigger, more visible walls in busy areas earn the most.

Who pays for the mural?

The sponsor does. They fund the artist, materials and equipment, and pay the rental to you. Listing and hosting a wall costs you nothing.

How long does the mural stay up?

Most sponsored murals run one to three months. Your agreement sets the dates and whether the mural stays, is repainted, or the wall is restored afterwards.

How do I list my wall?

Sign up free, then add your wall's address, size, photos and availability. It goes live for sponsors and artists to find, and you review every proposal before anything is agreed.

What happens when the campaign ends?

Whatever your agreement says: keep the mural, have the wall repainted or restored, or list it again for the next sponsor. Many owners keep the artwork.

Can I donate my wall instead of renting it?

Yes. Community programs, schools and nonprofit sponsors look for donated walls, and you choose the proposal you like. Ask your tax advisor about deductions for a donated wall.
